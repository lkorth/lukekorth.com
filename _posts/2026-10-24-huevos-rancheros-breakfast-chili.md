layout: recipe
kind: Recipe
dek: "A breakfast take on chili with potatoes, sausage, and scrambled eggs."
title: Huevos Rancheros Breakfast Chili
categories: [food, chili]
date: 2026-10-24
prep_time: "15 min"
cook_time: "1 hr 15 min"
total_time: "1 hr 30 min"
servings: "10"
ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, for the vegetables"
  - "1 yellow onion"
  - "1 poblano pepper"
  - "1 pinch salt"
  - "1 tsp bouillon paste"
  - "3 Yukon Gold potatoes"
  - "2 tbsp minced garlic"
  - "2 (15 oz) cans fire-roasted diced tomatoes"
  - "2 (15 oz) cans tomato sauce"
  - "6 oz diced green chiles"
  - "7 tsp chili powder (<a href=\"https://altonbrown.com/recipes/smoky-homemade-chili-powder/\">AB's Chili Powder</a>)"
  - "1 1/2 tbsp parsley"
  - "1 tbsp lime juice"
  - "1 tbsp Cholula hot sauce"
  - "1 tbsp onion powder"
  - "1 tbsp garlic powder"
  - "2 tbsp brown sugar"
  - "1 lb turkey sausage"
  - "3 tbsp butter, for the eggs"
  - "5 eggs"
  - "6 oz heavy whipping cream"
  - "1 green onion"
steps:
  - text: "Clean and dice the **onion** and **poblano pepper**. Slice the **green onion**, keeping the greens and whites together. Cut the **potatoes** into small cubes, around 3/8 inch."
  - text: "In a large, heavy pot, melt **1 stick of butter** over medium heat. Add the **onion** and **poblano pepper** along with a **pinch of salt** and cook until softened, stirring often."
  - text: "Add the **bouillon**, **potatoes**, and **minced garlic**. Cook while stirring for 1–2 minutes until incorporated and fragrant."
  - text: "Add the **diced tomatoes**, **tomato sauce**, **green chiles**, **chili powder**, **parsley**, **lime juice**, **Cholula**, **onion powder**, **garlic powder**, and **brown sugar**. Bring to a low simmer."
  - text: "In a separate pan, brown the **turkey sausage**."
  - text: "Add the browned sausage to the pot. Cover and maintain a simmer for 1 hour."
  - text: "In a separate pan, melt **3 tbsp of butter** and scramble the **eggs**."
  - text: "Add the eggs, **green onion**, and **heavy cream** to the pot. Adjust to taste: heavy cream and brown sugar to balance acidity, brown sugar for sweetness, salt for saltiness, chili powder or green chiles for spiciness. Continue to simmer with the lid off until it reaches the desired consistency."
  - text: "Serve and garnish with sour cream, shredded cheddar cheese or queso fresco/feta, and crackers as desired."
---
