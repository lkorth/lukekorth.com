ingredients:
  - "1/2 cup (1 stick) butter, for the vegetables"
  - "1 medium yellow onion"
  - "1 red bell pepper"
  - "3 tbsp butter, for browning"
  - "1 lb ground turkey"
  - "20 fl oz pale lager, such as Miller Genuine Draft"
  - "2 (28 oz) cans fire-roasted diced tomatoes"
  - "2 (28 oz) cans tomato sauce"
  - "1 (15 oz) can chili beans in chili sauce"
  - "1 (15 oz) can kidney beans"
  - "1 (15 oz) can black beans"
  - "1 (15 oz) can sweet corn"
  - "1 tbsp garlic powder"
  - "1 tbsp onion powder"
  - "10 tsp chili powder (<a href=\"https://altonbrown.com/recipes/smoky-homemade-chili-powder/\">AB's Chili Powder</a>)"
  - "1 tbsp ground cumin"
  - "1 tbsp smoked paprika"
  - "3/4 tsp cinnamon"
  - "1/2 tsp allspice"
  - "1/4 cup brown sugar"
  - "2–3 chipotle peppers, to taste"
steps:
  - text: "In a large, heavy pot, melt **1 stick of butter** over medium heat. Add the **onion** and **red bell pepper** and cook until softened, stirring often."
  - text: "In a separate pan over medium-high heat, brown **3 tbsp of butter**. Add the **turkey** after the butter is completely browned. Cook the turkey, breaking it up by stirring. When the turkey is almost fully cooked, remove any excess liquid, return to heat for a minute and deglaze with a splash of **beer**."
  - text: "Add the turkey, remaining liquid and all, to the pot with the onions and peppers. Add the remaining **beer** and bring to a rolling boil for several minutes."
  - text: "Add the **diced tomatoes**, **tomato sauce**, **chili beans**, **kidney beans**, **black beans**, **corn**, **garlic powder**, **onion powder**, **chili powder**, **cumin**, **smoked paprika**, **cinnamon**, **allspice**, **brown sugar**, and **chipotle peppers** to the pot. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to maintain a simmer and simmer for 1 hour with the lid on."
  - text: "Remove the lid and allow to thicken to the desired consistency. Adjust to taste: powdered broth or seasoning salt for salt, brown sugar for sweetness, chipotle peppers for spiciness."
  - text: "Serve and top with additions of choice. Sour cream, shredded Wisconsin cheddar cheese and hearty crackers are all good choices. Wisconsin cheese curds and cheddar ranch biscuits are also a great combination. Cinnamon rolls work well too."

This started out as [Hy-Vee's Wisconsin Badger Red Chili](https://www.hy-vee.com/discover/recipes/wisconsin-badger-red-chili) and has been perfected over years and dozens of
iterations. Extremely well balanced, rich, and just a little sweet.

Just about any meat works well, provided the excess fat and grease are drained. We've made it with
everything from ground venison to sliced cheddar brats, but typically use ground turkey (do not skip browning the butter).
